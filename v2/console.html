Building the Ramp Advisor Console

Strategy • Visual design • SAAS • Product research • MVP
Context

Role

Only designer with 1 PM and 5 developers, late 2022 to early 2023 (~2 months).

Task: Take the Advisor Console from early design stages to GA launch while building & iterating on core features.

Context: Established companies have grown through accounting channel. Ramp wanted to leverage strong accounting partnership to launch a product making accountants' lives easier, motivating them to bring other clients to Ramp ecosystem.

Solution preview

Problem

What problem are we trying to solve?

Managing clients, accountants, and roles gets cumbersome for admins with multiple clients.

Ramp Advisor Console: One-stop-shop to manage permissions, team, & clients.

Key features: Navigate between client accounts in seconds, assign staff to specific clients, refer clients directly in the Console.

Problem, opportunity, and context

Core Feature #1

Building core feature #1 — Action Items

Relevant and actionable data-points from each client's Ramp account, surfaced on the console.

Primary goal: Give users ability to centralize insights across clients without clicking into each account.

Target Persona

Michelle

Accountant Admin for a firm managing 25+ clients

"I need to know if/when my accountants have closed the books and what are the top things/clients that need my attention."
