Designing for Growth

Ramp Experimentation Data-Driven A/B Testing

Overview

Worked with the Growth team at Ramp to design experiments and touchpoints to boost user-acquisition across the funnel. This case study is a compilation of several growth projects, each contributing to acquisition in varying capacity.

Impact

Each project boosted user-acquisition in varying capacity across different funnel stages.

My Role

Partnered with growth PMs and data scientists to identify high-leverage opportunities. Designed and shipped rapid experiments, analyzed results, and iterated based on quantitative outcomes.

Key Design Decisions

  • Designed multiple landing page variants optimized for different acquisition channels
  • Created onboarding experiments that reduced drop-off at key conversion points
  • Built reusable experiment templates to accelerate the team's testing velocity
  • Used data to challenge assumptions and let metrics guide design direction
