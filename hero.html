Building a daily super-app

Hero Founding Designer Mobile Novel Interactions

Overview

As the Founding Designer at Hero, I designed the entire product and brand experience. The app features natural language voice interactions and a bundling of multiple apps into a single, seamless experience, helping users run their day — a first in the industry.

Impact

World-class retention and strong product-market fit signals.

My Role

I was responsible for end-to-end product design — from initial concept and brand identity through to interaction design and production-ready assets. I worked closely with the founding team to define the product vision and translate it into a polished, shippable experience.

Key Design Decisions

  • Designed a natural language voice interface that makes complex daily tasks feel effortless
  • Created a unified app experience that bundles multiple utilities without cognitive overload
  • Built a brand system from scratch — including visual identity, motion language, and interaction patterns
  • Prioritized onboarding flows that drive immediate value and long-term retention
